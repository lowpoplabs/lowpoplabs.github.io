Four of them came off the Cobalt payroll and never left this island. One went up into the ice. One is out past the dust with the machines. One puts things back in the ground where it's still green. I'm not writing their names down. They didn't all leave clean, and two of them would come asking how I knew.

The fourth is redacted. She keeps a railcar lit out in the green and she doesn't mind being found — she'll take your scrap and pay it back in gear if you can work. Start with her. She's the only one who'll explain a thing twice.

How it works

A chain, not a menu

One trader will talk to you on day one. The other three won't give you their names until someone vouches for you. Finish a story to open the next door — and which door follows which changes with the season.

Hidden shops

Each trader runs a lit railcar shop placed at a random valid spot in her biome every wipe. Walk up to the counter and press E to talk — and she answers out loud. Four women, four voices, coming from somewhere behind the counter.

Real quest lines

Hunt, gather, build, and fight through hand-written quest chains with escalating gear rewards — from planters and workbenches to things that go through armour.

Buried caches

Every finale hands you a grid reference and a code to a locked stash hidden somewhere on the map. What's inside is worth the whole chain.

The island prints back

Somebody is still running a press. Anti-Cobalt pamphlets turn up in crates — recruitment sheets, guerrilla field notes, a defaced company memo — all signed THE UNPAID. Nobody has ever met a member. The sheets count the wipes.

The journal

Type /quests for a two-tab journal: The Work — what's active and what's next — and The Story — every chapter you've unlocked, re-readable any time.

The season rolls

Each wipe the island draws one of six storylines — who's hiding what, whose rumors are true, what the paper trail is even about. Same four women, never the same season twice.

It doesn't wait

Days into a wipe, new pages surface — sheets by a drop box, letters at a light somebody keeps, fresh prints joining the crates. Show up late and the story kept moving without you.

Somebody decides

Every storyline ends with a question at the vault, and the first to answer decides it for the whole island. The next wipe remembers what was chosen.

Just… talk to them

Every trader carries an AI-driven free chat — hit Chat in her dialog and type whatever you want. She knows who you are, what you're working on for her, and she remembers your last conversations — and you'll hear her say it, in her own voice. She will not tell you where the stashes are. People have tried. But do enough of her work and she warms up — and warm people let things slip.

How it works

Open the book

Type /sb anywhere. No item to craft, no slot to spend, nothing to lose when you die with your boots on. It was always in your head anyway.

Store pages

One button sweeps every pamphlet, photo and written note out of your inventory and pastes them in. The paper is consumed; the page is kept.

Two sections

Prints under one tab, papers under the other — each remembers where you left off. Notes lay out as readable pages: title, date, every word.

Take a copy

Pull any page back out as the real thing — a photo for a frame, a note word-for-word for whoever needs to read it. The book keeps the original. Always.

No duplicates

The book knows every print by its picture and every note by its words. Feed it the same one twice and it politely hands the spare back.

Yours alone

One book per player. Nobody pages through yours, nobody tears yours out. What you saved is between you and the island.

Changed your mind?

Every page has a Remove button. It asks twice — the first click arms it, the second one tears. Do anything else and it stands down. Nobody loses a keepsake to a misclick.

The wipe takes everything else

Your base, your gear, your grudges — gone on wipe day. The scrapbook isn't on the map, so the map can't take it. Every print you've saved, every numbered sheet from wipes past — still there next life. Some people keep a base. You keep the record.

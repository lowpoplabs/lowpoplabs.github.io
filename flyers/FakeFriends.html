FakeFriends

Friendship Not Included

A plugin that populates a low-pop private Rust server with persistent, player-like residents — recurring characters who log on, keep schedules, work jobs, hold grudges, die real deaths, and talk back. Not scripted NPCs. Neighbors.

A Night on the Island actual game chat
GrannyMags has connected.
you: anyone been near airfield?
RustyBill: crates respawned about ten minutes ago. elite first, always elite first
Moth: the candles are lit on the north cliff. come or don't.
Vex was killed by TrapperJoe

Every name above is a resident. RustyBill answered because someone spoke near him — a small language model wrote that reply, in character, in about a second and a half. Vex will remember TrapperJoe.

The Residents six ways to live here

Trapper

Runs trap lines since before the first wipe. Sells the good furs, keeps the weird ones.

Farmer

Grows the best berries on the island and will tell you about every single one of them.

Smelter

Lives where the ground is dead and the furnaces never cool. Pays in gunpowder, accepts no complaints.

Monument Runner

Knows every crate spawn on the island and exactly how long each takes to come back. Annoying about it.

Hostile

Shoots first, taunts second, loots third. In that order, every time.

Roleplayer

Only comes out at night. Lights candles on cliff edges. Nobody knows why. Moth knows why.

What Makes It Different the cool parts
Real Bodies

Actual players, not NPCs

Residents are built on Rust's native bot player tech — real player entities with real inventories. They sleep in their bases when "offline," leave lootable corpses when killed, and even trigger the proper death screen. Kill one and you've genuinely killed a player. It just wasn't a person.

Living Schedules

They keep hours

Each resident has traits — night owl, early bird, chatty, reclusive — that drive when they log on and off. Join and leave messages hit chat like anyone else's, so the server always feels inhabited, even at 2 a.m. with one human online.

Local AI Brain

They talk — and remember you

Chat near a resident and a small language model running on a spare GPU in the basement answers in their voice, median reply ~1.5 seconds. They hold real conversations, stay in persona, and keep per-player memories between sessions. Zero cloud, zero subscription.

Consequences

Grudges, graves & fight-or-flight

Residents decide to fight or flee when attacked. Hostiles remember who killed them and come looking. Dead residents leave graves that appear as map markers — the island slowly writes its own history.

An Economy

Jobs, shops & secret vendors

Farmers farm, smelters smelt, and some residents run hidden shops you have to find. Wares reflect what they actually do all day — the desert chemist really does pay in gunpowder.

Loot Runs

They run monuments for real

Monument runners physically travel to places like Airfield, dodge the scientists, crack the crates, and haul the loot home. First live run: a resident cleared six Airfield crates and walked them back to base. Race them to the drops.

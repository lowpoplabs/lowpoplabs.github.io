SCRAP4SCRAP

PAID BY THE KILO

A plugin that puts a scrap-metal dealer in the Junkyard. Haul in the metal everyone else walks past — ore, propane tanks, engine parts, the hatchet you finally broke — and the dealer weighs the load and pays scrap by the kilo. Every other plugin on the island takes your scrap. This is one of the few that hands some back, as long as you are willing to carry it there.

HOW IT WORKS ONE DEALER, ONE SET OF SCALES

THE DEALER

ONE NPC, ZERO VIOLENCE

An invincible, disarmed dealer stands in the yard (rust-coloured dot on your map). Walk up, press E, and the scales open: every line in your pack the yard buys, its weight and its payout, and a total at the bottom.

SELL THE LOT

ONE BUTTON, OR LINE BY LINE

Each line has its own SELL button and one button clears the lot. Payment is real scrap in your inventory, dropped at your feet if the pack is full. What you are wearing is never weighed.

THE TICKET

FRACTIONS CARRY OVER

Three quarters of a scrap is still yours. Whatever does not make a whole scrap stays on your ticket and is added to the next load, so a pocketful of blades is worth the walk.

END OF LIFE

BROKEN GEAR HAS A PRICE

Tools, weapons and armour are bought only once they are worn out, and never with attachments still fitted. A working rifle stays off the scales, so the lot button cannot eat it.

NO BUY-BACK

SOLD METAL IS GONE

The dealer has no stock and sells nothing. Everything bought is destroyed: metal leaves the island, scrap enters it. A daily limit per player (300 scrap by default) keeps the yard from being the only job in town.

SERVER OWNERS

ONE LEVER, AND A WARDROBE

Scrap per kilo moves everything priced by weight at once; any item can take a fixed price instead. Every weight is in the config. The dealer is placed with one command and returns to the same spot after a map wipe. Every piece of the dealer's outfit takes its own workshop skin. All text is translatable.

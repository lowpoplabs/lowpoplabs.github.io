Mechanics

Swipe, Don't Wait

The plugin runs the crate's own hacking routine and skips straight to the end. The unlock sound, the light, the map marker: all still fire. Everybody still knows. They're just fifteen minutes too late.

The Card Pays

Each swipe burns 25% of the card's condition. Four crates per fresh card, then it snaps. Found a card at 40%? That's one swipe and a souvenir.

Not Everywhere

Crates that belong to an event keep their full timer, and so does anything at Radtown. Cargo Ship and Oil Rig crates work by default; the server can turn either off.

Rules of the Reader

Stand within 3 m and actually look at the crate. Two-second cooldown between swipes, so a nervous trigger finger doesn't eat two cards. The server can raise the tier to a blue or red card, change the durability cost, or gate the whole thing behind lockedcratebypass.use. If a swipe does nothing, one of those is why.

Fifteen Minutes Back

Spend them on the loot instead of the wait. Green cards drop everywhere; hoard them like ammo, because on this island a keycard opens more than doors.

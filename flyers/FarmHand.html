Farm Hand

A worker posted at your tool cupboard, paid by the day in scrap. It picks, replants, waters and fertilises the planters while you're away, and sees to the chickens, bees, cows and sheep, the composter, the biofuel generator and the kitchen. You build the farm; the hand keeps it running.

Stand in your base · type /farmhand hire · press use on the hand.

Four hands, or one that does it all
/farmhand hire planter

Planter

The planter boxes

Picks what's ripe into your output boxes, pulls what's dying, waters each planter when it drops below its band, replants empty slots from the supply box and keeps a fertiliser in every planter with plants.

Eats one fruit for one seed to keep the seed stock you set.

/farmhand hire stockman

Stockman

Coop, hive & pasture

Feeds and waters the coops, pets the chickens, collects and hatches eggs to the flock size you set, empties the hives, keeps the troughs full, milks the cows and shears the sheep once they trust it.

Surplus is butchered if you say so. Breeds by the genes.

/farmhand hire yard

Yard

Composter, generator & kitchen

Plant fibre, spoiled food and the dung around the animals go into the composter; fertiliser comes out. Feeds, stirs and empties the biofuel generator.

With a cooking workbench: bread for the mangers, pies from your own pumpkin and beef, or teas from the berries.

/farmhand hire carter

Carter

The runs

Fetches water from the nearest river when the barrel runs low. Carts the surplus to the Farmers Market and brings the scrap back to your boxes, buying seeds there if asked.

Wool and spare animals go to the livestock vendor; calves and lambs come back.

/farmhand hire alone hires a hand that does the lot · up to four per farm · a farm at every base you own

Mechanics

Paid By The Day

100 scrap a day per hand, up to a week in advance. Pay at the Public Works office under MY ACCOUNTS, or by phone, or with /farmhand pay on a server without one. When the wage runs out the hand stops, waits two days, and leaves. Paid days stay on your account.

Honest Work

Every job uses the game's own code: the pick a player makes, the seed's own plant, a sprinkler's splash. One job every 15 seconds per hand, only on planters, coops, hives and boxes inside its cupboard and within 30 m of its post. Nothing is faked.

Can't Be Hurt

Turrets ignore it. Scientists, BetterNpc crews and the rest of the island's NPCs ignore it. It opens your doors while you're near and shuts them behind it; nobody else's. Away from you it works in place and never touches a door.

The Runs

When the barrel drops below a quarter the hand walks to the edge of the farm, is gone for as long as the round trip to the river would take, and comes back with 10,000 ml. When the boxes hold a load worth 50 scrap or more and a Farmers Market stall is in range, it takes the surplus to market under your daily limit and the market's own prices, and the scrap comes back with it. Tell it on the VENDOR tab and it will keep the herd up: calves 300, lambs 150, bought from the island's livestock vendor out of the takings.

Income With A Running Cost

The hand is paid out of what the farm makes. A farm bigger than one player can keep up with earns more with a hand than without; a window box does not. Build the farm first, then hire.

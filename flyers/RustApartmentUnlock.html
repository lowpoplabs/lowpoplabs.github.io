ApartmentComplexUnlock — Now Leasing
Rust server plugin · Oxide // now leasing // V 1.0.5

Apartment​ComplexUnlock

The Common Ground update built a monument you can rent but not live in. This plugin hands renters the keys: furnish your room with real deployables, run industrial pipelines, hang wallpaper, apply your skins — while management decorates the grounds with recyclers, car lifts, and an entire gambling wagon.

Furniture not included. That’s the point.

Posted in the lobby — notice to all tenants

House Rules — Building Management

  1. The rooftop security system (27 automated turrets) has been permanently decommissioned. Management regrets earlier incidents.
  2. Tenants may now furnish their own units. Hold the furniture; a ghost outline follows your eye. Green means yours. Red means not yours. Left-click sets it down. R turns it.
  3. Electricity is included in your rent. Do not ask where the wires are. There are no wires.
  4. Furnishings left behind at checkout are destroyed, not forwarded. Take your things before you leave — management refunds nothing after eviction.
  5. The grounds remain a safe zone unless management decides otherwise. What happens inside your unit is between you and your guests.

— MGMT

posted · lobby noticeboard

Every rule above is real plugin behavior. Read rule 4 twice.

Tenant amenities — what your rent gets you

Hold. Click. Home.

Hold any deployable item inside your rented room — box, rug, furnace, chicken coop — and a ghost outline follows your crosshair. Left-click places, R rotates. No commands. The item is consumed on placement and refunded when you /apundeploy.

Utilities included

The apartments have no power grid and wiring is impossible here, so the plugin force-powers what you place — the same trick the game uses for the built-in fridge. Lights, heaters, fridges, boom boxes just work.

Industrial plumbing

Hold the pipe tool: click a source, click a target — a real, functional pipe connects them. Storage adaptors snap onto containers, crafters onto workbenches, conveyors run on house power. Your room becomes a factory.

Wallpaper, both sides

Hold the wallpaper tool, pick a design in its menu, click a wall: a papered panel appears flush against it, design on both sides, at the vanilla cloth price. Click a panel again to re-paper.

Your skins, your stuff

Hold the spray can, click anything you placed — a skin picker opens. Only skins you own, verified with the game’s own ownership check. No exceptions, admins included, not configurable.

A real tenancy

Placed objects register as the game’s own apartment furniture: they survive restarts, get taxed like vanilla furnishings, lockable items auto-lock for the room’s renters — and checkout clears them out, exactly like the built-ins.

Grounds & management

Admins decorate the whole monument with the same hold-and-click flow, plus /apdeploy for the statics no player can craft: a recycler, a car lift, a horse trough — and a casino corner with slot machines, blackjack, and the entire gambling caboose, dragged off its rails and frozen in the courtyard.

/aplayout save snapshots the decor relative to the monument, and the saved layout re-applies itself on every boot — so when a forced wipe moves the building across the map, the furniture follows the lease.

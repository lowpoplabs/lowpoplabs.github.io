Mechanics

Rent By The Day

50 scrap buys a day. Pay at the Public Works office under MY ACCOUNTS, or ring the office from any telephone. You can stack up to 14 days ahead, so one trip covers two weeks.

Any Terminal

Lockers at the monuments are terminals, and monuments that never had one have had one installed. Look at it and press use. It opens your locker, all 48 slots of it, no matter which monument you're standing in.

Yours Alone

Nobody else can open it. Not your team, not the squad camping the door. It can't be raided, because it isn't anywhere. Getting home from the terminal is still your problem.

When The Rent Runs Out

The locker locks. Your gear stays inside, held until you pay again; one day of rent opens it back up. Type /locker any time to see how long you're paid for and where to pay. Lockers are emptied on map wipe, so clear yours out before wipe day; rent you've already paid carries over.

Travel Light

Farm one end of the island and bank it before the walk back. Stash a spare kit where you'll want it tomorrow. The road between two monuments is where loot goes missing, and now it doesn't have to be on it.

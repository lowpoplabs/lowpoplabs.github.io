RUST SERVER PLUGIN DEPT. OF ISLAND TRANSPORTATION V1.5.5

ISLANDTAXI

ANYWHERE ON THE ISLAND. NO QUESTIONS ASKED.

A plugin that puts a taxi company on the island. Pick up any phone — pay phone or mobile — and dial. A dispatcher answers, you pick your ride from an eight-vehicle fleet, drop a pin anywhere on your map, and the fare is quoted before you commit. Then your taxi actually arrives: flies, sails, or drives in from the horizon with a uniformed driver at the controls, picks you up, and takes you there. Cash up front. No refunds — island policy.

NEW THIS RELEASE V1.5 · SURGE PRICING, REAL BOAT ENGINES & POSTERS ON EVERY WALL

STREET TEAM

POSTERS ON EVERY WALL

The department's weathered recruitment posters now hang at bus stops and monuments across the island - sun-faded, rain-stained, stapled up slightly crooked, and repaint-proof (yes, even for admins). Server operators pick the walls with one command; one capture covers every copy of that monument. If you can read the number, you can call the cab.

SURGE PRICING

BUSY HOUR? BUSY METER.

When the island runs hot - three or more rides in an hour - fares climb in posted steps: +25%, +50%, +100%. The menu says so in orange before you pay, the posted rates already include it, and the quote you confirm is the price you're charged. Off-peak riders pay sticker. Supply, meet demand.

MARINE DIVISION

A REAL HAND ON THE THROTTLE

Water taxis are now driven, not towed: real throttle, real rudder, a real wake off the stern. You'll hear the engine idle at the dock and open up on the straights. The helmsman reads the water ahead, steers around icebergs, and ends every pickup with a proper straight-astern back-out before turning for open sea.

MARKED ZONES

MONUMENTS, BY APPOINTMENT

Named monuments are no-fly territory for freelance landings — the department doesn't put skids down inside a compound unless there's a marked taxi zone. Where one exists, pin anywhere nearby and your taxi sets down exactly on the mark. Server operators walk the island and stamp the zones themselves; one mark covers every copy of that monument, wipe after wipe.

OIL RIG

BY AIR AND BY SEA

Both oil rigs are on the route map: helicopter service to the helipad deck, and a boat dock at the boarding ladders — the RHIB noses up to the ladder, holds station while you climb off, then backs straight out to sea. Pickups too: call from the rig and your ride meets you at the mark. The only taxi company with an offshore division.

EXECUTIVE

ARMED AND ARMORED

The Attack Heli seats you behind a company M249 with bottomless ammo, puts HV rockets on your trigger, and pops real flares the instant a homing launcher locks on. Prefer the ground? The Armored Car wraps you and the driver in armor plate, outruns every other car on the island, and hides the radio in the engine bay. Same price. Choose your armor.

TEAM RIDES

THE WHOLE CREW, ONE FARE

Call a Scrap Heli and teammates standing near you are seated automatically when you board — with a last call before liftoff for stragglers. Everyone rides, everyone arrives, everyone gets out together. Herding your team to one rooftop is now the taxi's problem.

FLIGHT SAFETY

CLEARED DESCENT CORRIDORS

Every landing zone is checked with a helicopter-sized sweep from cruise altitude to the ground — rooftops, overhangs, and walls are rejected before you're ever charged. Pilots follow the real terrain, buildings included, and a taxi that can't finish the job sets down safely and tells you so.

THE SERVICE STANDARDS OF THE DEPARTMENT

SAM CLEARANCE

Aerial taxis broadcast department transponder codes — SAM sites hold fire on the route, yours included. Clearance beats route planning.

UNIFORMED DRIVERS

Every taxi is driven by a uniformed professional — cap, shades, boots. Unarmed, courteous, and utterly indifferent to whatever you're fleeing.

NIGHT SERVICE

Cars, bikes, and boats run lights after dark, dawn to dusk service either way. Boat fares ride up front, out of the glare of the stern light.

WATCH IT ARRIVE

No taxi materializes in front of you. Yours is dispatched 400 m out and travels the whole way in — listen for rotors, hoofbeats, or an engine on the road.

RADIO CABS

Premium cars carry a working dash radio — it clicks on when you board. The driver does not take requests.

CASH OR CREDIT

Economics and ServerRewards balances accepted where installed; plain scrap everywhere else. Fares quoted up front, charged on confirmation.

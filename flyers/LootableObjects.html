Lootable Objects

The island is full of things that should have parts inside them and never did. Now they do. Walk up to a substation transformer, press E, and it opens like a crate: fuses, scrap, tech trash, metal fragments, the odd switch or splitter, and once in a while a high-grade fuse. Empty it and it refills in half an hour.

If it hums, it probably has a fuse in it.

Mechanics

Press E

Look at a transformer from within 4 m and press USE. A loot panel opens, same as any box. Take what you want, close it, walk away. No tools, no timer, no hacking.

Rolled Fresh

Each fill draws 2 to 5 items from the loot table. Scrap and fuses are common, tech trash and metal fragments show up often, switches, branches and splitters now and then, and a high-grade fuse, a medium battery or a large solar panel about one time in twenty.

It Comes Back

Empty a transformer and it goes dark for 30 minutes, then restocks. Leave something in it and it stays as you left it. First one back after the cooldown gets the fresh fill.

Anything Can Be A Crate

Transformers are the default, not the limit. The server can make any static world object lootable: point an entry at a piece of the object's name, give it a capacity, a cooldown and a loot table, and it opens. Admins find the names with /lootobj debug, which prints what you're looking at into chat. Nothing is saved to the map, nothing can be picked up, and it all cleans up when the plugin unloads.

Hit The Substations

Every power substation on the island has transformers. Every transformer has parts. Fuses run the monuments, and now you know where they've been hiding.

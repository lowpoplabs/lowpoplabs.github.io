RUST SERVER PLUGIN DEPT. OF ISLAND INFRASTRUCTURE V2.8.1

PUBLICWORKS

YOUR SCRAP AT WORK

A plugin that opens a Public Works office on the island. One clerk, nine municipal utilities, one shared bucket per service. Anyone can walk in and pay 100 scrap to bank a day of service — power, water, gas, cold groceries, free trains, Cobalt air cover — for everyone on the server. The more people using it, the faster the meter runs. Things break: transformers blow, mains burst, pumps seize — and somebody has to go fix them. The shops are open: fuel pumps and a corner store at every gas station, groceries and drone delivery at the supermarket — and yes, the vodka is drinkable. Civilization, by the day, cash only.

THE SERVICES 100 SCRAP / DAY EACH · ONE SHARED METER PER SERVICE

ELECTRICITY

The Power Plant and every street powerline stay live — and pole transformers put out double power while the bill is paid. Wire your base to the grid.

WATER

Water Treatment stays pressurized. Tanks full, valves open, no gears required.

GAS

All three Dome crude pumps run continuously — no oil rig field trip. Each pump holds 500 crude, then waits for someone to come collect.

MARKETS

Freezers stay cold, the grocery store is open, and delivery drones fly from the supermarket roof. The island's only full-service shop.

GARAGES

Gas station lifts and doors stay powered — the fuel pumps work and the corner store is open. Bring a hose tool, leave with a full tank and snacks.

AIRPORT

The control tower is staffed and powered, no fuses — airdrops run at double rate and a Chinook resupply is one button press away, every ten minutes.

INTERNET

The satellite uplink stays online and item-free. No tech trash, no aiming modules, free thrusters.

TRANSPORTATION

Every train on the island runs without fuel. Board, throttle, go. The subway is now public transit.

PROTECTION

The patrol heli and Bradley go reactive only — they ignore everyone who leaves them alone, and Bradley never drops its ground crew. Shoot first, and Cobalt remembers.

BUDGET CUTS V2.7.7 · NON-ESSENTIAL SERVICES

NON-ESSENTIAL

COBALT SAYS NO

Server operators can now shelve any of the nine services from the config. A shelved service stays on the office board — grayed out, no gauge, no PAY button — stamped "Cobalt has deemed this service non-essential". The clerk won't take your scrap for it, and the fault scheduler leaves it alone. Head office has spoken.

BUDGET FREEZE

THE METER STOPS. THE MONEY KEEPS.

Time already banked on a shelved service is frozen, not refunded — no drain, no expiry, no faults. Reinstate the service and the meter picks up exactly where it left off. (And since v2.7.8, a typo in the config can't wipe your settings — the department refuses to overwrite a broken file and runs on defaults until it's fixed.)

AIR TRAFFIC CONTROL V2.7.5 · THE TOWER IS STAFFED

NO FUSES

THE LIGHTS ARE ON UPSTAIRS

Vanilla wants two fuses in the tower's fuse boxes before either terminal wakes up. While Airport is paid the department wires the terminals directly and seats a locked Public Works Fuse in each box — no sparks, screens lit, meters full. The supply dispatcher stays active (airdrops at 2× rate, always) and the resupply terminal is charged and ready. Pull the fuse? "Item is locked in."

ON BREAK

NEXT LOCKED CRATE IN 10 MINUTES

Press the button and a Chinook brings a locked crate to the airfield — then the air traffic controller takes a 10-minute break, announced island-wide with the minutes left. Vanilla would lock the terminal for three hours after one call; the department cuts that to a coffee. Lapse the bill and the tower goes dark, fuses and all, back to vanilla.

AIR COVER V2.7 · COBALT DEFENCE, BY SUBSCRIPTION

REACTIVE ONLY

THE HELI IGNORES YOU. UNTIL.

Paid up, the patrol helicopter and the Bradley APC only engage aggressors. Walk under the heli with a rifle out, drive past the tank at Launch Site — nothing. Hooks-only, no convars, no saved state: the second the bill lapses, both are pure vanilla again.

HOSTILE

TWO WAYS TO GET FLAGGED

Put a round into the heli or the Bradley, or hit another player within 150 m of either one, and Cobalt flags you hostile for 2 minutes — refreshed every time you do it again; dying settles it. Both vehicles share the list. Chat tells you exactly why.

YOUR TEAM

CONSEQUENCES FOR EVERYONE

When you get flagged, your whole team gets flagged. Your clanmate starts a fight under the heli, the heli starts a fight with you. Hostile players are still subject to the vanilla rules — line of sight, the 40 m night rule — the service only ever removes targets, never adds them.

BILLING HOLD

THE FAULT IS AN INVOICE

Protection's fault has no crash site — Cobalt Accounts puts the account on hold. Minor: payment delayed, air cover lapses (heli vanilla, Bradley still tame). Major: account frozen, both vanilla. The red dot lands on the office; hit PAY BILL there — 50 / 100 scrap, no payout. Nobody gets paid by Cobalt for paying Cobalt.

OPEN FOR BUSINESS V2.4–2.5 · THE SHOPS ACTUALLY WORK

FUEL PUMPS

PUMPS THAT PUMP

A row of three working fuel pumps behind every gas station building while Garages is paid (the ones out front are scenery — the real pumps are round the back). They fill with low grade around the clock (250 cap each) — hold a hose tool and press E to drain one into your inventory. First one there gets the tank.

GROCERIES

THE STORE IS OPEN

A Public Works Grocery inside every supermarket while Markets is paid — eggs, bread, apples, honey — and a corner store at every gas station while Garages is: chocolate, granola, canned goods. Each shop stocks its own shelf, restocked by the department. Scrap spent here leaves the economy — a bill you can eat.

DRONE DELIVERY

AIR MAIL, BUT GROCERIES

A real drone marketplace operates from the supermarket roof — order from any vending machine on the island and a department drone flies it in. Same machinery as the fishing villages, now municipal.

BOTTOM SHELF

THE VODKA IS REAL /drink

The Mr Spice Can and Vodka Bottle junk items are actually drinkable now — type /drink, or put one on your belt, holster, and right-click to swig. Spice heals and hydrates; vodka heals harder, blurs your vision for 20 seconds, and carries a 15% chance the batch is bad: you throw it straight back up — no buzz, no heal, pickle-jar roulette — plus real snakebite venom, straight off the snake. Drink responsibly. Or don't.

CLOSING TIME

NO BILL, NO SERVICE

Stop paying and the shops stay standing but close — pumps idle, the grocers turn you away, the drone terminals go dark. A minor fault grounds the drones first; a major one shutters the whole store. The office takes scrap.

STOCKED BY CONFIG

THE DEPARTMENT SETS PRICES

Every shop's shelf is a config list — seven items per machine (the game's own vending limit), each a shortname, quantity, and scrap price, with separate lists for the supermarket and the gas stations. Ship fresh produce, or run a state liquor store. Purchases restock themselves every five minutes.

FAULT EVENTS SINCE V2 · MUNICIPAL DECAY, SIMULATED

SOMETHING BREAKS

ABOUT ONCE A DAY, SOMETHING BREAKS

A transformer blows on the grid, a water main bursts, a signal box fails at the Train Yard. Random faults hit active services only: minor faults cut output to 50% — half pump flow, drones grounded — major faults go full blackout. The meter keeps running either way — that's the pressure.

OPEN CONTRACT

TAKE THE JOB. IT'S A RACE.

The office posts one open repair contract per fault. Anyone can accept it; first to finish the repair gets paid — 75 scrap minor, 150 major, plus bonus hours banked on the fixed service. Everyone else gets exercise.

REPAIR RUN

BRING PARTS, PRESS E

A red dot marks the fault site. Haul the materials — metal fragments, gears, fuses, pipe — across the island and press E at the site. Dying on the way with a backpack full of gears is part of the fee.

THE CREW

THE DEPARTMENT FIXES IT — EVENTUALLY

Ignore a fault and the Public Works crew repairs it themselves in 45–90 minutes. Nothing stays broken forever. Heroes just get there first — and get paid.

HOW IT WORKS MUNICIPAL BUREAUCRACY, SIMULATED

THE CLERK

ONE NPC, ZERO VIOLENCE

An invincible, disarmed, AI-disabled clerk staffs the office (orange dot on your map). Walk up, press E, and the services panel opens — status lights, time remaining, and a PAY button per service. Away from the office, /pwinfo shows every meter, read-only. Paying your bill still takes the trip.

THE BUCKET ECONOMY

ONE BUCKET, DRAINED BY DEMAND

Every service is a shared bucket of banked time — strangers' payments pour into the same bucket, capped at 7 days. Two players sip at 1×; every extra player online opens the tap wider (+25%, capped at 3×). The panel shows fuel-gauge bars and time left at current usage, so everyone knows when to pass the hat. Bots don't count. Payers get named.

REAL MACHINERY

BUILT ON THE ACTUAL POWER GRID

Services drive the real Power Trip monument machinery — the same generators, pumps, and freezers players power with heavy fuses. The fuel pumps are the Dome's own crude machinery, retargeted. Vanilla fuse gameplay keeps working alongside; when a service lapses, everything reverts to vanilla on its own.

NO LOOPHOLES

THE FUEL HATCH IS SEALED

Free trains could have been an infinite low-grade faucet. Instead, fuel hatches are sealed while Transportation is active, tanks refill the instant anyone boards, and surplus fuel is drained back when the service expires. The gas station pumps cap at 250 fuel and the Dome at 500 crude — nothing accumulates forever.

UTILITY NOTICES

WARNINGS BEFORE THE LIGHTS GO OUT

Chat announcements fire when a service activates, 60 and 10 minutes before it lapses, and when it finally expires. The panel shows banked time against the cap — 4d 12h / 7d — so everyone knows exactly when to pass the hat.

THE OFFICE

A REAL OFFICE, WITH A SOUNDTRACK

The department lives in a real monument office (currently Radtown), anchored so it re-opens in the same room after every map wipe — as do the pumps, the grocery, and the drone pad. The clerk wears a proper uniform, turns to greet you, and an indestructible boombox plays office radio around the clock — staff retune it through the radio dial, civilians get told it's department property. Every message is translatable via the Lang API. CopyPaste support included for custom-built branch offices.

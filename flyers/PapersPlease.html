Papers, Please

Cobalt has set up checkpoints on the island's roads and at its monuments, and it keeps a file on everyone. Behave and the guards wave you through. Rob its crates, shoot its scientists or raid your neighbours and the same guards open fire. The longer the wipe runs, the tighter the grip, until Outpost is a police state.

Type /papers to read your file. Then decide how you want it to read.

How Cobalt runs the island

Checkpoints

Gates at monuments, roadblocks on the roads, two-guard patrols on foot. Run one and the whole island hears about it, and the guards shoot you on sight for a while.

Papers

/papers request at a checkpoint gets you a Cobalt ID, which softens a search by one band. Carry someone else's and it gets seized.

The Fence

A man at Bandit Camp launders [COBALT] loot and sells forged papers. A forgery works until a guard spots it. After tier 3, being caught makes you Wanted.

The Vault

Outpost has a guarded vault with elite crates stocked by tier. No papers open it, not even a Citizen's. It takes three C4, and C4 won't arm inside a safe zone…

Sabotage

…unless you buy a bypass tool from the fence and flip the substation switch. The safe zone drops, the sentries go dark, and a response squad comes looking for whoever did it.

The Ambush

An Enemy of the State who gets spotted draws a hunter squad from out of sight. If they put you down, they don't finish you. They arrest you: stripped, cuffed, hooded, and left on the beach.

Earn It Back

Surrender contraband, pay your fines, and let time pass: your score drifts toward zero. Or take a Public Works repair contract, worth +10 or +20.

Island Taxi

The taxi company checks your file too. Citizens ride 10% cheaper, the Wanted pay double, and Enemies walk.

Ask /help

The full field guide is in /help. It shows your server's real thresholds, fees and rewards, because they're read straight from its config.

What Gets You A File

Killing Cobalt scientists or guards, looting Cobalt crates, raiding other players' bases (even a wall you only damaged), running a checkpoint, and sneaking into a gated monument without going through the gate. Cobalt forgets slowly. Cobalt does not forgive a vault.

Your Record Precedes You

Every gate, patrol, fence, vault and taxi on the island reads the same file. Keep it clean and the island is yours. Burn it and Cobalt will find you.

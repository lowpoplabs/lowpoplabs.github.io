Mechanics

Quality Pays

The seed merchant reads genes. Every G and Y on a clone adds 25%, so a GGGYYY clone sells for two and a half times a WXWXWX one. Breeding finally has a buyer.

Variety Pays

Dump a mountain of one crop and the price slides, down to half. It climbs back over the day. The glut is shared by the whole island, so grow what your neighbours aren't.

Daily Limit

The stalls pay each player up to 300 scrap a real-time day, counted from your first sale. The panel shows what's left. Past that, they're not buying until tomorrow.

Small Change

Produce is priced in fractions of a scrap, and nothing is rounded away: whatever doesn't make a whole scrap is carried to your next sale. Sell one item with SELL or the lot with SELL EVERYTHING; the scrap lands in your inventory. Type /market for where the stalls are and how much they'll still pay you today.

Steady Money

The power bill is due, the locker rent is due, and the taxi doesn't run on goodwill. A few planters and a chicken coop will cover a day or two of it, every day, without anyone having to die for it.
